      Quality Commitment · Separate hand-offs

      Each of the three commitments remains its own contract and accountable responsibility.

      Values and Q: related, not dependent

      Values Commitment expresses the standards the company chooses to pursue and the business actions needed to pursue them.

      Quality Commitment defines how to measure customer experience, audits and quality/productivity goals.

      Quality findings may inform value reviews; ethical commitments may inform KPI selection. Neither manager automatically approves or scores the other.

      KAI·ROI v1 · View the protected formal equations

      Official formal structure — author: PhD Jorge Lucio. This section is a faithful display, not an executable calculation.

      KAI_i* = φ_i · u_i · f_i · ψ_i · SPO_i · P_i · Γ_g(i),t
ψ_i = promedio(DataActivation_i, I_net_i)
SPO_i = CC_i · ABCD_i · NPS_i
MD_i = Σ_s (I_i,s · R_i,s + E_i,s · Q_i,s)
CE_i = ((((KAI_i* · MD_i) - C_i) / C_i) - WACC_t) / WACC_t
CE_empresa = Σ_i CE_i
V%CE = (100 / N) · Σ_i 1(CE_i > 0)

      Separate operational layer (non-substitutive): portfolio health Γ is supported by validated LTV and retention; retention = 1 − churn. Productivity of people and organisational resources lives in P_i. A diagnostic φ_SWOT is not a direct formal multiplier. Unknown data are never replaced by zero.

      The formal equation is sovereign. Implementation is subordinate. Operational interpretation never replaces the formal core.
